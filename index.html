About Us

Evidence from the Earth.

Evidence from the Earth is a knowledge and research-focused platform dedicated to building a meaningful connection between nature, traditional knowledge, and modern scientific research.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a digital knowledge platform where nature-based knowledge and modern science can connect.

We aim to help document traditional and local knowledge, understand medicinal plants and their traditional uses, and support the discovery of new research questions for future researchers.

Our long-term vision is to encourage responsible scientific exploration of potentially valuable natural compounds while respecting traditional knowledge, biodiversity, and the rights of local communities.

Why Do We Do This?

Many communities possess valuable traditional knowledge about plants and natural resources. However, this knowledge is often passed down through oral traditions, local practices, or scattered sources.

The important point is that traditional use and scientifically proven medical effectiveness are not the same thing. Therefore, responsibly documenting this knowledge and organizing it for research is important.

Evidence from the Earth was created to help address this gap by presenting natural and traditional knowledge in a structured digital environment and encouraging responsible scientific curiosity.

Artemisinin derivatives and antimalarial activity: mechanisms of action

Dr. Maya Chen · Institute for Botanical Science · 18 Aug 2026

Research summary

This article examines how artemisinin derivatives interact with parasite biology, bringing together laboratory observations and a clear account of the questions that remain open.

Scientific integrity note: Information presented represents preliminary laboratory findings and should not be interpreted as established medical advice.

Objectives & background

The study maps candidate mechanisms across parasite life stages and places current findings in the wider history of plant-derived antimalarial research.

Methodology

Researchers combined comparative cell assays, compound profiling, and a review of peer-reviewed evidence. Results were interpreted as exploratory signals rather than clinical outcomes.

Key findings & data

Several derivatives showed distinct activity profiles in controlled laboratory conditions. Further validation, reproducibility studies, and clinical research are needed.

Potential applications in modern medicine

The findings may inform future compound design and responsible research questions, while making no claim about treatment or individual health outcomes.
